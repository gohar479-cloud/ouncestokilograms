About Ounces to Kilograms

Who we are and why you can trust our conversions.

Our mission

OuncesToKilograms.com is a free online tool that converts ounces to kilograms the moment you type a value, for both the standard (avoirdupois) ounce and the troy ounce used for precious metals. Our goal is simple: give everyone fast, exact, and trustworthy weight conversions, with the reference material to back them up.

People use ounces on US labels, parcels, and recipes, while most of the world uses kilograms. We exist to make moving between the two effortless and accurate.

Accuracy you can rely on

Every conversion factor, chart value, and worked example on this site is based on the exact figures published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), not rounded approximations. One avoirdupois ounce is exactly 0.028349523125 kg, and one troy ounce is exactly 0.0311034768 kg. These values are fixed by law and do not change from year to year.

Who writes our content

Our guide is written and reviewed by Gary Bennett, a writer and metrologist who specializes in measurement and unit-conversion content. You can read more about his background and review process on his writer page and in our editorial policy.
