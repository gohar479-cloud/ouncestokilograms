About Gary

Gary Bennett is a metrologist, a specialist in the science of measurement. He writes and reviews unit-conversion and measurement reference guides, with a focus on getting every factor, definition, and worked example exactly right.

His work centers on turning official measurement standards into clear, accurate tools that ordinary readers can trust, whether they are converting a recipe, a parcel weight, a newborn's weight, or a bar of gold.

What Gary reviewed on this site

For the Ounces to Kilograms Converter, Gary checked each conversion factor, every value in the 36-row chart, and all of the worked examples against the exact figures published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

He confirmed both the avoirdupois ounce (0.028349523125 kg) used for everyday weights and the troy ounce (0.0311034768 kg) used in the tool's Troy setting for gold, silver, and other precious metals.
