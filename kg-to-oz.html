What Are a Kilogram and an Ounce?

A kilogram is the metric base unit of mass equal to 1,000 grams. An ounce is a US customary unit equal to 28.35 grams, and the troy ounce for metals weighs 31.10 grams.

The Kilogram

The kilogram (kg) is the base unit of mass in the International System of Units (SI) and the everyday weight unit in most countries. Since 20 May 2019, the kilogram has rested on the Planck constant, a fixed number from physics. Its size stayed the same, so every ounce answer stayed the same too. Bags of rice in Lahore, flour in Berlin, and luggage in Dubai all carry kilogram labels.

The Ounce and the Troy Ounce

The avoirdupois ounce (oz) is a unit of mass that equals one sixteenth of a pound and is used to weigh food, mail, and people in the United States. The troy ounce (oz t) is a heavier unit of 31.1034768 grams used only for gold, silver, platinum, and palladium. The full history of both ounces lives on the ounces to kilograms.

Why Is the Factor 35.27396195?

The factor is the reverse of the ounce definition. One ounce weighs 0.028349523125 kg, so one kilogram holds 1 ÷ 0.028349523125 ounces.

1 ÷ 0.028349523125 = 35.27396195

A second route uses pounds and reaches the same number. One kilogram equals 2.20462262 pounds, and each pound holds 16 ounces. So 2.20462262 × 16 = 35.274 ounces.

What Is the Step-by-Step Method?

To convert kg to oz by hand, follow 4 steps: write the kilograms, multiply by 35.274, round, and check. The whole method takes less than a minute with a phone calculator.

  1. Write the weight in kilograms as a decimal number, such as 3 or 0.45.
  2. Multiply that number by 35.274, or by 35.27396195 for lab work.
  3. Round the answer to fit your task, such as 1 decimal place for cooking.
  4. Check the answer against a known value, such as 1 kg = 35.27 oz.

Here is the method with a real number. Three kilograms times 35.274 equals 105.82 ounces. Three times 35.27 is close to 106, so the answer passes the check.

From the Kitchen Kilogram to the Gold Kilobar

Every answer so far uses the grocery ounce of 28.35 grams, which suits flour, luggage, parcels, and pets. Precious metals use a heavier ounce with its own name. A kilogram of flour and a kilogram of gold weigh exactly the same, but the gold kilogram is counted in a larger ounce, which gives a neat, smaller number. How Many Troy Ounces Are in a Kilogram?

One kilogram equals 32.1507 troy ounces. To convert kg to troy oz, multiply the kilograms by 32.1507466.

oz t = kg × 32.1507466

The troy ounce weighs 31.1034768 grams, so each kilogram holds a tidy 32.15 of them. The LBMA lists the same factor, 32.1507465 troy ounces per kilogram, for the London bullion market. Pick the Troy setting in the converter to use this factor.

Gold investors meet this number on every kilobar. The 1 kg gold to oz answer is 32.15 troy ounces, and silver works the same way. Dealers commonly print "oz" on bars as a short form of troy ounces.

How Many Fluid Ounces of Water Are in a Kilogram?

One kilogram of water fills about 33.81 US fluid ounces. A fluid ounce measures volume, so this answer applies to water and liquids with a similar density.

To convert a kilogram of water to fluid ounces, multiply the kilograms of water by 33.814. Half a kilogram of water fills 16.9 fluid ounces, the size of a standard water bottle. A 1.5 kg bottle of water holds about 50.7 fluid ounces.

Other liquids follow their own density, which is mass per milliliter. Cooking oil is lighter than water, so a kilogram fills a little more space. Honey is denser, so a kilogram fits neatly into a smaller jar. The converter's ounce result stays a weight, so it suits any liquid on a scale.
