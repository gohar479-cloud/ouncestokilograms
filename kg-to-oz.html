Quick fact. One kilogram is 35.274 ounces, which is a little over two pounds (2.2046 lb). So a 1 kg bag of flour weighs about 35 ounces, and a 5 kg dumbbell is roughly 176 ounces.

How Do You Convert Kilograms to Ounces?

Multiply the number of kilograms by 35.27396195 to get ounces. Dividing the kilograms by 0.028349523125 gives the same result.

Formula

oz = kg × 35.273961951 kilogram = 35.27396195 ounces (exact)

Example

2 kg × 35.27396195 = 70.548 ozSo 2 kilograms equals about 70.55 ounces.

For precious metals, multiply kilograms by 32.1507 instead to get troy ounces. A 1 kg gold bar is 32.1507 oz t, which is why bullion is often sold as a "kilobar."

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ounces are in a kilogram?
One kilogram equals about 35.274 ounces. The exact value is 35.27396195 oz.
How do I convert kilograms to ounces?
Multiply the number of kilograms by 35.27396195. For example, 3 kg × 35.27396195 = 105.82 oz.
How many kilograms is 1 ounce?
One ounce equals 0.0283 kg, or exactly 0.028349523125 kg. To go from ounces back to kilograms, multiply by that number.
How many troy ounces are in a kilogram?
One kilogram equals 32.1507 troy ounces. Use the Troy setting for gold, silver, platinum, and palladium.
Is this ounces by weight or fluid ounces?
This converter uses ounces by weight, known as avoirdupois ounces. Fluid ounces measure volume, not weight.
How many ounces is 2 kg?
Two kilograms equal 70.55 ounces (70.54792 oz), which is about 4.41 pounds.
